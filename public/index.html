About

Small on purpose

TraderShelf is an independent project maintained by one person. There is no account system, no analytics on your inputs, and no premium tier — the tools are the whole product. If a calculator can run locally, it should.

The shelf grows slowly. Somewhere near the front of the queue: a fee-aware break-even calculator and a plain-text trade journal you can export.
